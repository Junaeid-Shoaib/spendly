Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you register, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you log, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to run your account, show your spending summaries and breakdowns, and improve Spendly. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and kept only as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data and expense records within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
